The schedule

Voyages depart every 2 hours, on odd hours JST — which is even hours UTC. Both families follow a deterministic 144-voyage (12-day) cycle, so they can be computed exactly rather than guessed.

Indigo — Eorzea

vn = unix_ms // 7_200_000
route = INDIGO[(88 + vn) % 144]

Destinations B Bloodbrine Sea · T Rothlyt Sound · N Northern Strait of Merlthor · R Rhotano Sea; times D/S/N. Each 12-entry day-block is the previous block rotated left by one.

Ruby — Far East

vn = unix_ms // 7_200_000
route = RUBY[vn % 144]

Nine routes = 3 voyage paths × 3 time-variants. The three paths are One River, Ruby Price and Thavnairian coast. Ships from Kugane (needs Stormblood); the Thavnairian coast also needs Endwalker.
