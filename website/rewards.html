Rewards & bonuses

Your catch points are multiplied by the voyage's content bonuses — objectives completed by the boat as a whole, the star rating of those objectives, and whether a party of three or more is aboard.

The rules: 105 % / 110 % / 120 % for completing 1 / 2 / 3 mission objectives · 150 % when a party of 3+ clears all ★★★★★ objectives · +130 % for the shark / octopus / fugu / crab category goals.
